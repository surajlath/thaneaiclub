Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. The café charges only for what you order.

I'm a beginner. Will I fit in?

Yes. Half the room uses AI at work without building anything. The clinic table is there for exactly that.

Can I show something I built?

Please do. Reply DEMO in the WhatsApp group; 5 minutes on screen, any stage, any tool.

Do I need a laptop?

No. Bring one only if you're demoing or want help at the clinic.

How often?

Every other Sunday. After 29 Nov: 13 Dec and 10 Jan.
